# PlayVortex Chat Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

PlayVortex Chat is a third-party Chrome extension that adds a friends-only messaging interface to PlayVortex.

PlayVortex Chat is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by PlayVortex.

## Information We Handle

To provide the chat feature, PlayVortex Chat and its chat backend may handle the following information:

- **PlayVortex account information:** your PlayVortex user ID and username.
- **Friend information:** information needed to display your PlayVortex friends, including friend IDs, usernames, online status, and avatar URLs.
- **Personal communications:** messages that you send and receive through PlayVortex Chat.
- **Conversation information:** conversation IDs and timestamps associated with messages.

## How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide the PlayVortex Chat service.

This includes:

- Displaying your current PlayVortex account.
- Displaying your PlayVortex friends.
- Showing friend avatars and online status.
- Creating and identifying conversations.
- Sending messages to conversation participants.
- Storing and retrieving conversation history.
- Delivering messages to connected users in real time.

We do not use this information for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling.

## Where Information Is Sent

The extension communicates with:

1. **PlayVortex** to obtain the currently signed-in user's account information and friend-related information needed by the extension.
2. **The PlayVortex Chat backend** to create conversations, send messages, retrieve message history, and deliver messages in real time.

The PlayVortex Chat backend is hosted using Cloudflare services.

## Message Storage

Messages sent through PlayVortex Chat are stored by the PlayVortex Chat backend so that conversation history can be retrieved.

Stored message data may include:

- Message ID
- Conversation ID
- Sender's PlayVortex user ID
- Message text
- Message timestamp

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or chat messages.

We do not share collected information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information is transmitted to the PlayVortex Chat backend when necessary to provide the chat functionality.

Messages are delivered to the participants of the conversation in which they were sent.

## Cookies and Authentication

PlayVortex Chat does not request your PlayVortex password.

The extension uses your existing signed-in PlayVortex session in the PlayVortex website to obtain the account and friend information required by the chat interface.

Do not provide your PlayVortex password, cookies, session tokens, or other private credentials to anyone claiming to represent PlayVortex Chat.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Conversation messages may remain stored on the PlayVortex Chat backend so that conversation history can continue to work across browser sessions.

At this time, PlayVortex Chat does not provide an automated self-service message deletion feature.

For questions about stored data or a deletion request, please contact the project owner through the project's public GitHub repository.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the chat service and its stored data. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

You should avoid sending highly sensitive information through chat.

## Children's Privacy

PlayVortex Chat is intended for users who are permitted to use the service under the applicable rules of PlayVortex and the Chrome Web Store.

The extension is not intended to collect information from children for advertising or unrelated profiling.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data handling changes.

The latest version will be published in this GitHub repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about PlayVortex Chat, please use the public GitHub repository associated with this project.

**GitHub:** https://github.com/igakojp/playvortex-chat

## Third-Party Services

PlayVortex Chat depends on PlayVortex and Cloudflare services to provide its functionality. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply to your use of those services.
